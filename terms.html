2. Nature of Wholesale Order Requests

Submitting an order form through the Wholesale Portal represents an order request and an inquiry for billing invoice generation. Orders are scheduled for botanical production and fulfillment upon verification and payment of the issued invoice.

Wholesale accounts and wholesale dispensary tiers are reserved exclusively for licensed or certified perinatal healthcare providers (including certified midwives, licensed acupuncturists, doulas, and birth clinics). Smooth Birth formulations are traditional East Asian botanical infusions, decoctions, and nourishment designed to support the body’s natural physiologic rhythms during pregnancy, birth preparation, labor, and the postpartum transition. Practitioners utilize their independent clinical judgment when administering or recommending botanical formulations in their practices.
