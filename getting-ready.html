Getting Ready

General Birth Preparation & Pelvic Relaxation

1 fl oz (30 ml) Strictly 39+ Weeks

The final weeks of pregnancy can feel heavy, tight, and stagnant. Getting Ready is a targeted, late-term herbal formula designed to encourage a spontaneous vaginal birth. By utilizing botanicals that actively expand and relax the abdomen and pelvis, this blend relieves late-pregnancy physical stagnation, soothes uterine muscles, and clears the energetic pathway for labor to begin naturally.

Administration & Timing
Best Taken: Strictly from 39 weeks onward. (Do not begin prior to 39 weeks).
Dosing: At 39 weeks: 2 droppers twice a day. At 40 weeks (past due): 4 droppers twice a day. SROM/Induction: 7 droppers every 2-3 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clinical FAQ for Getting Ready

Is this formula right for me?

If you have reached the 39-week mark and want to proactively prepare your body for a smooth, spontaneous labor, this is for you! It is especially helpful if you are feeling a lot of heavy, stagnant pelvic pressure or uterine tightness without productive contractions.

How do I actually measure a "full dropper"?

We keep it simple: One firm squeeze of the rubber bulb = 1 full dropper. Don't worry about trying to fill the glass tube all the way to the top! One good squeeze will fill the glass tube about halfway up. This equals exactly 1 ml. So, for a 2-dropper dose, just give the bulb two firm squeezes into your water or juice.

Will I notice any positive side benefits while taking this?

Yes! Because this formula excels at improving circulation and releasing tension, many mothers report feeling less "heavy" in their pelvis, a reduction in restless leg discomfort, and a general sense of relaxation in their lower abdomen as their body softens for birth.

Are there any negative side effects I should watch out for?

This is a very gentle, food-grade formula. However, because its job is to move blood and energy downward to prepare for birth, it may increase mild uterine cramping (which is the goal!). If you experience any unexpected heavy spotting or bleeding, stop taking the herbs and contact your midwife or OB-GYN.

Can I take this with my other medications?

As with any herbal supplement, you should always clear it with your midwife or doctor first. Because Dang Gui and Chuan Xiong naturally improve blood circulation, you should consult your provider if you are currently taking prescription blood thinners.

I’m pregnant. Should I be worried about the alcohol in the tincture?

Not at all! We use organic cane alcohol because it is the absolute best way to extract the active medicine from the herbs, but the amount you consume in a dose is microscopic (similar to the alcohol naturally found in a ripe banana). However, if you prefer to avoid alcohol completely, simply drop your dose into two ounces of hot water, wait 5 minutes, and the alcohol will completely evaporate before you drink it.

I got into labor before I finished this bottle. What a waste!

No worries. Getting Ready can be used during labor for labor boost. If you take it during postpartum, it will help lochia discharge.
