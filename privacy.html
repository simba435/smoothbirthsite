Privacy & Data Stewardship

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Smooth Birth Apothecary LLC collects information when birth practitioners register for wholesale dispensary access, submit order requests, or communicate with us. This information includes:

  • Full Name and Professional Title
  • Practice, Clinic, or Business Name
  • Professional Credentials (State License Number, NPI, or Doula Organization)
  • Email Address and Telephone Number
  • Shipping and Practice Billing Addresses

2. How We Use Your Information

We use contact and clinic details solely to:

  • Verify practitioner eligibility for professional wholesale dispensary pricing
  • Issue official billing invoices via secure processors (Square or QuickBooks)
  • Coordinate botanical fulfillment and direct shipping to your practice
  • Respond to formulation inquiries and provide tracking updates

3. Data Protection & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or clinic data. Details are transmitted strictly to operational infrastructure partners (such as secure form gateways, PCI-compliant invoicing processors, and couriers like FedEx and UPS for tracking).

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update your records, please email hello@smoothbirth.com.
